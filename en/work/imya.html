A mark from a bubble and smoke

Identity for a private hookah chat on Telegram, the main one in St Petersburg. The clients were its two founders, who also run the chat. The chat already had a star and the colour yellow; the brief was to lock them in and grow conversation inside the chat. The logo is a chat bubble plus smoke, and the asterisks in the name work both as censorship and as a mark. I added some attitude on top: lines from the chat went onto T-shirts and hoodies.
