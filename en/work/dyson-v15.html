DYSON_V15_/_01

Looks clean. Isn’t.

An ad concept for Dyson V15 Detect. The vacuum’s laser reveals dust the eye can’t see. I made that proof the hero of the whole campaign: billboards, social, email.

DYSON_V15_/_02

Discount instead of proof

As of 5 November 2026, Dyson runs 63 active ads in the US Meta Ad Library, 12 of them discount-led. Handheld vacuums get three ads — V8, V10 and V12 Detect — and all three sell on price. The V15 and its laser don’t appear at all. Dyson shows hidden dirt only for the R3 robot, with UV light, in six ads.

In Reddit reviews, 11 comments ask whether it’s worth the money or better to wait for a sale. 16 are about the pleasure of seeing the result, 7 of them about the laser and the particle counter. Four describe the shock when the laser found dirt on a “clean” floor.

Insight: a floor that looks clean isn’t necessarily clean. The V15 laser shows it.

System rules: one proof per frame, the laser is the brightest light in the frame, headlines of three words or fewer, no discounts in the frame.

DYSON_V15_/_03

How to test it

Test: the laser creative against Dyson’s current price creative, one audience, 14 days. Success threshold: +20% CTR at 50,000+ impressions per creative. Next metric: cost per add-to-cart.

Basis: at RAWMID, a new visual language for product cards lifted CTR from 1.7% to 3.4% at equal impressions.

This is a hypothesis; the test has not been run. Speculative concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by Dyson.
